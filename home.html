Home, made thoughtful

Use energy and resources wisely.

A sustainable home is not necessarily a perfect or expensive home. Start with maintenance, habits and changes that suit your space.

Save energy without losing comfort

Heating, cooling, lighting and appliances all use energy. The best opportunities depend on your home and climate, but everyday habits are a useful starting point.

  • Use daylight when it is available and switch lights off in empty rooms.
  • Wash suitable clothes at lower temperatures and wait for a full load.
  • Air-dry laundry when practical.
  • Turn off devices rather than leaving them running unnecessarily.
  • Keep doors and windows closed when heating or cooling a room.

Save water thoughtfully

Take shorter showers where practical, fix dripping taps, and run dishwashers or washing machines with suitable full loads. Use water-saving settings if your appliances have them.

Buy less, choose better

Before replacing an item, ask whether it can be repaired, borrowed or bought second-hand. When a purchase is necessary, durability, repairability and how often it will be used can matter more than the packaging.

Renting? Focus on changes you can control, such as switching off unused devices, reducing food waste, draught-proofing only where permitted, and discussing larger efficiency improvements with your landlord.
