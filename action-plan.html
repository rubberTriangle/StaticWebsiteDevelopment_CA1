Keep it achievable

Your first-week sustainability plan.

Use this simple guide to turn good intentions into habits. No forms, sign-ups or special purchases required.

Seven small steps

A week of practical progress

Read through the ideas and choose the ones that fit your circumstances. This is a guide, not a test.

DAY 01

Notice

Spot one thing you often waste: food, electricity, water, packaging or time spent travelling.

DAY 02

Plan

Make a shopping list or meal plan using things you already have.

DAY 03

Save energy

Choose one simple energy habit, such as switching off unused lights and devices.

DAY 04

Reuse

Repair, borrow, refill or reuse something instead of buying a replacement.

DAY 05

Travel

Consider a practical alternative for one journey, if it is safe and accessible.

DAY 06

Share

Exchange a useful tip with a friend, family member or community group.

DAY 07

Reflect

Keep the habit that felt most useful. Adjust or drop anything unrealistic.

ANY DAY

Learn more

Use trusted sources to check claims and learn about the bigger picture.

Explore further

Reliable places to learn

Sustainability is a broad topic, and advice can change with local rules and new evidence. These organisations provide accessible background information.

  • United Nations — Sustainable Development Goals. An overview of global goals covering climate, consumption, health, cities and more. Visit the UN goals website.
  • United Nations Environment Programme. Information on resource use, pollution, climate and sustainable lifestyles. Visit UNEP.
  • European Environment Agency. European environmental information, indicators and explainers. Visit the EEA.
  • Citizens Information (Ireland). Practical public information, including household waste and recycling topics. Visit Citizens Information.

Code and feature references

The website's HTML and CSS are written specifically for this project. The two JavaScript extensions added to the original site are listed below, along with references explaining the methods used.

  • JavaScript extension 1 — Animated hamburger menu. Clicking the three-bar menu button opens or closes the mobile navigation. JavaScript handles the click event, and a short fade-and-slide animation makes the menu appear and disappear. MDN: Element.animate() and MDN: addEventListener().
  • JavaScript extension 2 — Sustainable tip button. On the Everyday habits page, clicking “Show me a tip” displays a sustainable tip: “Turn off lights when you leave a room.” The click event updates the text on the page using JavaScript. MDN: textContent.

Photography is loaded from Unsplash image URLs. Image sources are credited by their hosted URLs; photos are modified only through CSS effects such as masks, blending and filters.

A gentle reminder

Consistency beats perfection.

Choose actions that are safe, affordable and suitable for your circumstances. If a tip does not work for you, try another.
