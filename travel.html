Choose your route

Move through the world with care.

Travel choices depend on distance, accessibility, safety, weather, time and local transport. The most sustainable option is one that works in real life.

For everyday journeys

Consider the options

You do not need to change every journey. Think about the trips where an alternative is practical.

Walk or cycle

For short, safe routes, active travel can support wellbeing and reduce fuel use. Check route safety and accessibility first.

Use shared transport

Where available, buses and trains can reduce the number of individual car trips. Check timetables and plan connections.

Make car trips count

Combine errands, share journeys when convenient, and keep tyres properly inflated according to vehicle guidance.

For longer trips

When planning a holiday or work journey, compare the full journey rather than only the headline transport mode. Distance, occupancy, route, and the number of connections all matter. If a lower-impact option is practical and accessible, consider choosing it.

Make a realistic travel swap

Pick one regular trip and test an alternative for a week. Leave enough time, check the route in advance, and note what made the journey easier or harder. If the alternative does not work for you, use what you learned to try a different option.

Access and safety come first. Walking, cycling or public transport will not be suitable for every person or every journey. Sustainable choices should respect individual needs and local conditions.
