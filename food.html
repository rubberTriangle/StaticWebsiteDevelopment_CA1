Shop with intention

Good food habits, less waste.

What we buy, how we store it and what we eat can affect waste, land use, emissions and our household budget.

A more mindful kitchen

Make food go further

These steps can save money as well as resources. Choose the ones that suit your household and local food options.

01 / PLAN

Check before you shop

Look in the fridge and cupboards first. Plan a few meals around ingredients you already have, then write a short list.

02 / STORE

Keep food fresh

Store ingredients appropriately, freeze what you will not use in time, and label leftovers with the date.

03 / EAT

Explore plant-rich meals

Try adding beans, lentils, vegetables, nuts or grains to meals. You can begin with one plant-based meal each week.

04 / CHOOSE

Buy for usefulness

Choose seasonal or locally available food when practical, and consider packaging, price, nutrition and storage life together.

Shopping without the pressure

Sustainability labels can be confusing, and no single label tells the whole story. Focus first on practical basics: buy what you can use, favour durable goods, avoid unnecessary packaging where possible, and look for clear information from trusted sources.

Make leftovers part of the plan

Rather than treating leftovers as an afterthought, plan how they will be used. Roasted vegetables can become lunch, cooked grains can form a quick bowl, and suitable surplus portions can be frozen for busy days.

Food safety matters. Follow storage guidance and use your judgement. Sustainability should never mean eating food that may be unsafe.
