Start where you are

Everyday habits that make a difference.

Sustainable living is less about buying new “eco” things and more about using what you have thoughtfully.

Try the “one change” approach

When advice feels overwhelming, pick one action that fits your existing routine. Repeat it until it feels normal, then decide whether to add another. A consistent small action is often more useful than a big change that is difficult to maintain.

Five habits to start today

  • Carry a reusable bottle or cup if you will use it repeatedly.
  • Switch off lights and devices when they are not needed.
  • Keep a short list before shopping to avoid impulse purchases.
  • Repair, borrow or buy second-hand before buying something new.
  • Separate waste according to your local recycling rules.
Remember: Reusables are most helpful when they are used many times. You do not need to throw away useful items just to replace them with a more sustainable-looking version.

Common questions

Do I need to buy special sustainable products?

No. Using what you already own, repairing items and avoiding unnecessary purchases are often sensible first steps.

What if I cannot make every change?

That is normal. Focus on choices that are safe, affordable and practical for your circumstances.

How can I tell whether a claim is trustworthy?

Look for clear evidence, transparent methods and reliable sources rather than relying on vague labels or dramatic promises.

Make it stick

Connect a new habit to something you already do. Put your reusable bag beside your keys, plan leftovers while clearing dinner, or check the weather before deciding how to travel. Small reminders can remove friction.

Think beyond individual choices

Community repair events, local clean-ups, sharing schemes and asking organisations to reduce waste can extend the impact beyond your own household.

A quick reflection

Ask yourself

  • What do I use every day?
  • What do I often waste?
  • Which change is easiest to repeat?

The best first step is specific, affordable and realistic for you.
