A useful place to begin

Make sustainability practical

Sustainability means meeting today's needs while protecting the ability of future generations to meet theirs. Everyday choices are one part of that bigger picture.

Eat with care

Plan meals, store food well, and enjoy more plant-rich dishes without chasing perfection.

Food and shopping

Use less at home

Make energy and water savings part of your routine, and choose durable items where possible.

A greener home

Move thoughtfully

For suitable journeys, walking, cycling and public transport can reduce your travel footprint.

See it differently

There is no single “perfect” way to live sustainably.

Your options depend on where you live, your budget, your health, your time and what is available locally. Start with a change that feels possible, then build from there.
